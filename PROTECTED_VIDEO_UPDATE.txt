Adil Academy Protected Video Player Update v22

Changes:
- Replaces direct YouTube/Vimeo controls with Adil Academy custom play/pause/fullscreen controls.
- Blocks normal iframe clicking and right-click copying from the embedded player.
- Hides provider controls, share UI, keyboard controls, and direct fullscreen button.
- Adds a moving student account watermark to discourage screen recording/sharing.
- Applies protection in My Course and Q & A retake player.

Important:
This prevents easy/casual link copying. No browser-based YouTube embed can guarantee 100% protection from a technically skilled user using developer tools.
