ADIL ACADEMY — DAILY VIDEO LIMIT UPDATE

Behavior:
1. Student gets maximum 3 new lessons per Sri Lanka calendar day.
2. The first currently eligible lesson is visible/unlocked.
3. Lesson 2 unlocks only after Lesson 1 video reaches 100% and its Q&A has a score of 75% or higher.
4. Lesson 3 unlocks only after Lesson 2 meets the same rule.
5. If Q&A is below 75%, the next lesson stays locked. Existing retake rule remains: rewatch at least 50% of the same video before another Q&A attempt.
6. After 3 new lessons are passed for the first time on the same day, the next new lesson remains locked until the next Sri Lanka calendar day.
7. Previously passed lessons remain accessible for review.
8. Half-payment lesson access limits remain in force.
9. No database migration is required; the system uses existing lesson progress and lesson Q&A attempt records.
